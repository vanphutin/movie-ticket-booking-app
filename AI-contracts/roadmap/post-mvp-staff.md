# Post-MVP Staff route

Status: `NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_MVP`
Source: CCR-005; `API-TKT-004..006`

The three Staff endpoints are contracted to remove ambiguity but have no active MVP
ticket. Opening this route requires a future approved change/ticket that supplies:

1. Staff identity provisioning and least-privilege RBAC/deny matrix.
2. Ticket lookup privacy policy and check-in concurrency/state transition design.
3. Audit/retention model, expected-files manifest and reproducible verification.

Completion of ticket issuance does not authorize Staff check-in.
