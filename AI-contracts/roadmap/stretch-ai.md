# Stretch AI route

Status: `NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_MVP`
Source: CCR-005; `API-AI-001..010`

All ten AI endpoints are contracted as `STRETCH`; no current MVP ticket maps to them.
They may be proposed only after all core release blockers are clear. A future reviewed
route must decide ownership and introduce capability/tickets for:

1. Semantic search and embedding lifecycle.
2. Recommendation, preference and feedback privacy.
3. Admin drafting/apply workflow, human approval and provenance.
4. Abuse/cost limits, prompt injection/data-egress controls, usage/audit retention.

Contract presence is not implementation permission and does not count toward MVP release.
